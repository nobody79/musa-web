The difference

Google Maps indexes the city so you can consume it. MUSA indexes it so you can understand it.

Index of usefulness

“Where can I eat nearby?”

Google Maps organises the city around eating, shopping and sleeping: businesses and services.

Index of meaning

“Where did Quevedo live?”

MUSA indexes what each corner means — houses, tombs, stages, venues — filtered by what matters to you.

How it works

You choose, you walk, and the city starts talking.

01

Pick a passion

The whole map rearranges itself around it: not services, but places that mean something.

02

Follow a route, or build your own

Follow one of 182 narrated routes, or put your own together: tell MUSA how long you have and where to start.

03

Your travel notebook, with stamps

Every place you visit earns its stamp. Add photos and notes, and your city becomes a collection.

Along the way

Everything that walks with you.

MUSA doesn't stop at a place's page: it gets you there, speaks up when you arrive, and keeps a record of what you found.

New

Get directions, and see what's on your way

Pick a place and MUSA plots the walking route and hands it over to Apple Maps or Google Maps; for public transport, your maps app takes over. With “You'll pass by…” you see the places that match your passions along the way and add them as stops.

The route is free · “You'll pass by…” is Premium

FAQ

What people usually ask.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use MUSA, and even subscribe, without creating an account. An account is optional: it saves your notebook in the cloud so it follows you to a new phone.

What's free, and what's Premium?

Free: all 1,396 places and their stories, most of them with narration, the map, your travel notebook, 2 narrated routes per city, MUSA in your pocket and the “On this day” notifications. Premium opens the other 140 narrated routes, custom routes, the day plan and “You'll pass by…”.

How much is Premium, and how do I cancel?

€4.99 a month with 7 days free, or €39.99 a year. No commitment: you manage the subscription in the App Store (Settings › your name › Subscriptions) and can cancel whenever you like. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged. The App Store shows the price in your own currency.

What languages is it in?

The app is in 17 languages. Stories and routes are complete in Spanish, English, French, German, Portuguese and Italian, and narration is in Spanish and English.

Does it work offline?

Partly. Places and routes are stored inside the app, in Spanish, and open even with no signal; in other languages the translation needs a connection. Voices, photos and street-by-street walking routes need one too.

Which cities does it cover?

21 cities: Madrid, New York, Paris, Córdoba, Seville, Rome, Barcelona, Valencia, Málaga, London, Dublin, Amsterdam, Prague, Budapest, Zagreb, Istanbul, Ankara, Miami, Orlando, Los Angeles, and Las Vegas. More are on the way.

What about Android?

Coming soon to Google Play. There's no date yet: for now MUSA is for iPhone only.

What do you do with my location and my data?

You can use MUSA anonymously, and your data is never sold. Location is only used if you allow it, and MUSA in your pocket does the matching on your phone itself: it never stores or sends your route. It's all spelled out in the privacy policy, and you can delete your account at any time.
